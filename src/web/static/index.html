Best first. A trip counts as belonging to a tier only when it both leaves from and returns to that tier — flying out of Prague and home into Frankfurt still strands you in Frankfurt. Volatility probe

Three fixed routes sampled every two hours, to decide whether daily sweeping is often enough. Net move is first observation to last — the figure that says whether a fare is running away from you. Steps that moved counts any change at all, including the site rounding by a few crowns; the column beside it counts only moves worth noticing, and the gap between the two is why the steadiest route here used to report the highest volatility.

Where the prices come from

Sites move their URLs and rename their CSS classes, and either one stops the sweep without stopping the code. Both are strings, and both are here — edit the one that broke, press Test this source to run a single real search, and read what it parsed. Zero cards matched means the markup changed; a page that will not load at all means the URL did. Leave a field alone and it keeps the built-in value, so deleting data/sources.json always restores working defaults.
